`grilling` documents its form-based round against what `grilling_form` actually enforces: the call must be the only and final one of the round, a rejected round leaves the turn open so the model fixes `violations` and re-calls, `number` and `header` shape the heading rather than the order, and the answers arrive as a marked block in the user's next message. The user-facing text no longer hard-codes the plugin's UI copy, the field names are pinned to the instruction so a question stays in business language, the pre-selected recommendation carries the "agreeing never means answering no" rule that the old prose wording did, the stop-and-wait step no longer reads as a contradiction, and the closing conditions sit in one language. Its docs page is re-synced to the form-based round.
